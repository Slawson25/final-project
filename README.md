Project Name: Disney Fan Page
Project URL: https://youtu.be/o_c-qcUisqE

Description:
    The name of the Project is Disney Fan Page. The page is to let individuals get a glimpse of the story behind
the development of at the time 2 of the biggest parks, Magic Kingdom at Walt Disney World. It is a simple page
that will let those that visit get the curiousity and look into the park itself.

    I wrote a couple of files for the site. I input epcot.html, and magic.html. Both are meant to take the person/visitor
the stories that were produced about both parks on the Youtube platform. I wanted to include a picture of Walt himself
so I had to go to pexel website to get a free to use photo. For the magic.html site that was wrote, since it was for a
building of a park I wanted to see if I could find a blueprint or layout for the park itself. So since I did not want the
website to be an identical copy, I included a discontinued ride character instead of the original logo from the official
site itself.
    For the 4 other parks themselves I wanted to give the visitors of the site a visual of what other parks existed
from California, Flordia, and even to Shanghai. The content boxes that was made for these pictures, I am really proud
of how they turned out. I have had a hard time previously with getting these correct. With some research from W3Schools, Youtube,
and even going through Co-Pilot for more information on content boxes, all the sites assisted with getting the images and
the boxes just right.
    I did however on the main page of the website and index.html page include a video from file from youtube about the
park itself. It is a short 1:31 video that is used as a short commerical.
